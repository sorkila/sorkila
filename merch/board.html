Sólhvarf

Nine concepts for the rebirth shirt. One black metal signature, two rougher cousins, a quiet one for the office, and five that leave the genre on purpose. Each sheet has a layout schematic, specs, an AD note and a flat-lay prompt. Below the concepts: field notes from the bands that actually wear this genre, and a prompt pack with art-only, on-body and detail prompts for Grok Imagine.

The brief, as answered
What is rebornThe entire product. Not a redesign. A burn and a rebuild.
Who wears itInternal. Designers and engineers who get the reference. The logo may be unreadable. The rest must not be.
SolsticeThe new design system. Live, built by Erik. We print the record that exists, not a tour announcement.
LanguageNorwegian, Old Norse or Swedish. Old Norse where it reads as scripture, Norwegian or Swedish where people have to say it in standup.
Three rules I am directing by
  1. One unreadable thing per shirt. Szpajdel built seven thousand logos on bilateral symmetry and one principle: the logo can be a thicket, but the album title, the tracklist and the imprint stay legible. The shirt is a poster that happens to be cotton.
  2. The sun is a disc, never a wheel. The solstice sun sits half-sunk on a ridge, thin rays only upward. A closed wheel of bent spokes is the sonnenrad, and it has already cost one band a shirt in 2025. We do not go near it.
  3. The lyrics are true. Every song title is something this team actually did. Wry, not jokey. No puns, no "404", no "it works on my machine".

The lore

One band, one album, one label. Every concept pulls from the same record so the range feels like one band's merch table, not nine ideas.

    Field notes: the actual bands

    What real black metal merch does, band by band, and what each one hands us. The pattern across all of it: one ink, a hand-drawn logo, the record on the back, and a longsleeve with sleeve prints as the collector's piece. Colour appears only when the album cover had it.

    DarkthroneTransilvanian Hunger, 1994Fenriz with a candelabra, blown out to pure black and white, the cover on the front and "Ferdasyn" on the back, one ink. The most bootlegged shirt in the genre is a photocopy. We take: for 02, the high-contrast photo is the whole trick. One ink, nothing else.
    MayhemDe Mysteriis Dom Sathanas, 1994Nidaros cathedral in cold blue. The official longsleeve prints in white and purple, the one classic black metal shirt with a second cold colour, and only because the cover had it. We take: a second colour is allowed when the source owns it. That is the licence for 07's acid green.
    EmperorIn the Nightside Eclipse, 1994Necrolord's castle painting on the front, the title alone on the back, still sold as a longsleeve thirty years on. The Emperor logo is Szpajdel's, the most symmetric in the genre. We take: image front, title back, and the logo is the brand, not the company.
    Jannicke Wiese-HansenImmortal, Satyricon, early BurzumA Bergen tattoo artist drew the first Immortal logo by hand because a friend asked. The early covers she worked on leaned on Theodor Kittelsen, Norway's painter of plague, trolls and the far-off golden castle in Soria Moria Slott. We take: hand, never font. And Kittelsen's boy looking at the light across the valley is our sun on the ridge. Cite the painter, never that band.
    BathoryUnder the Sign of the Black Mark, 1987 · Blood Fire Death, 1988The goat logo on the back, the cover on the front, prints on both sleeves. Blood Fire Death put Peter Nicolai Arbo's Åsgårdsreien, a Norwegian painting from 1872, on a metal record. We take: the three-location longsleeve format for 08, and public-domain Nordic painting as a legitimate source.
    DissectionStorm of the Light's Bane, 1995Necrolord's reaper on horseback in blue snow. One painting carries the whole identity, and the back patch is the item collectors chase. We take: one image, no more. The woven back patch in the range is not an afterthought.
    UlverBergtatt, Kveldssanger, Nattens Madrigal, 1995 to 1997Three records in archaic Norwegian spelling ("Aatte Hymne til Ulven i Manden"), the third deliberately raw, the second acoustic and quiet. Tania Stene's cover art. We take: old spelling as texture, and the permission for 03 to be the quiet record in the catalogue.
    EnslavedFrost, 1994 · Eld, 1997Runes and Viking-age material done by Norwegians who read the sources, which is why it never looks like a costume. We take: 05's runes get transliterated by someone who knows the rules, or not at all.
    MgłaExercises in Futility, 2015 · Age of Excuse, 2019No faces, hoods, grey instead of white, a lowercase gothic "mgła" and almost no text. The whole identity is withholding. We take: 03's restraint, and grey as an ink option when bone reads too bright.
    Watain, MetastazisValnoir, ParisSigils, heraldry, baroque engraving, gold. A 2010 poster screen-printed in human blood. Valnoir's rule: if the concept of a cover fits in one sentence, you have won. We take: every concept on this board has a one-sentence thesis for that reason. Gold stays the only metallic.
    DeafheavenSunbather, 2013 · Nick SteinhardtA black metal record in pink and orange, with a custom "beyond high contrast" typeface the band later sold as a font. The proof that the genre survives leaving its palette. We take: 04 sets SOLSTICE in the system's own face, and the Sólhvarf logo ships as a glyph in Solstice when this is over.
    Wolves in the Throne RoomCascadian black metalForests, moons, owls, earth tones, nature photography instead of corpse paint. We take: 09's earth palette and the right to put a landscape on a shirt without a logo over it.
    Heilung, WardrunaLifa, 2018 · Kvitravn, 2021Antler, bone, sepia, drums, runes. Merch sold as artefacts, Lifa on black or white cotton. The genre most prone to borrowed marks. We take: 09's natural blank and brush texture, and the rule that nothing on it is a symbol.
    BatushkaLitourgiya, 2015Orthodox icon painting, Cyrillic wordmark, gold and red on black. A non-Latin script as the logo. We take: 05's runic front is the same move. Gold and oxblood on black is proven in the field.
    The formats, distilled

    Tee. Logo or image on the front, the record on the back. One ink on the classics. The imprint small at the hem, the way a label would print it.

    Longsleeve. Cover front, logo or title back, text or pattern down both sleeves. The piece people trade thirty years later. Concept 08.

    Back patch. The one image, woven, 30 cm. The item with no company on it at all. In the range.

    Colour. White on black until the cover says otherwise. Blue for Mayhem and Dissection, purple for one longsleeve, gold for Watain and Batushka. Nobody uses two accents.

    The concepts

    Schematics show print placement only. Grok draws the art. Where a concept depends on small type, render the blank garment and composite real type on top. Image models still misspell.

    Prompt pack

    Every concept, four prompts: the art alone for compositing, the flat lay, the shirt on a body, a macro of the print. Written the way Grok Imagine wants it: subject, then layout, then the exact words in quotes, then style and light, then the line that stops it adding text.

    Settings. Use Quality mode, the standard tier misspells. Art-only prompts at 3:4, flat lays at 16:9, on-body at 4:5, macros at 1:1. Four generations per prompt, keep one.

    Text. Only short words in quotes render reliably. The logo, the album title and the imprint are the words we trust Grok with. The tracklist and the token legend never render correctly, so for 03, 04 and 08 generate the art without them and typeset in Figma. Inspect every character before anything ships.

    Fixing a render. Upload the image and edit: Change the text to read exactly "SÓLHVARF". Keep the lettering style, placement, colours and everything else identical. One change per edit.

    Rejecting a render. Any skull, pentagram, inverted cross, sun wheel, horned helmet or recognisable band logo means prompt again, not retouch.

    Beyond the tee

    A band has a merch table, not one shirt. Pick two of these to go with the tee and the range reads as real.

    Back patchWoven, 30 cm, the Sólhvarf logo alone in bone on black. The one item engineers will actually sew on a jacket.
    BeanieBlack rib, the sun-on-ridge mark embroidered small in gold thread. No text. The midwinter drop item.
    Enamel pinThe sun disc at its lowest point, 22 mm, black nickel. Lapel-sized rebirth.
    Cassette stickerA J-card layout: band, album, tracklist, "Rec. Stockholm". For laptops. Costs nothing, lands everywhere.
    A2 posterThe tracklist as a tour poster, bone on black, one gold rule. For the office wall above the design system.
    ToteNatural canvas, the logo in black. The one piece non-believers will carry.

    Do not

    • No sonnenrad, black sun, wolfsangel or othala rune, and no closed wheel of bent spokes that reads like one. Danzig printed a sunwheel variant on a shirt in 2025 and spent the year explaining it.
    • No inverted crosses, pentagrams or goat heads. They are not our story and they are the lazy version of the genre.
    • No lifted band logos or fonts. If Grok produces something that looks like Darkthrone's or Emperor's logo, throw it away and prompt again.
    • No pun shirts and no English developer jokes. The wryness lives in true song titles, not in gags.
    • No company logo on the front. The imprint is small, on the back, with the year, and that is all.

    Production notes

    Garment. Oversized, boxy, heavyweight 240 to 250 gsm, garment-dyed washed black. The wash is what makes it read as vintage merch instead of a company tee. Longsleeve for the tour concept, raw ecru for the midwinter one.

    Print. Water-based or discharge screen print, so the ink sits in the cotton with no plastic hand. Bone white is the base ink on every concept. One accent colour per shirt, never two. Metallic gold only on the sun disc, where the site's gold edge lives.

    Palette. Bone #e8e6e1 is the site's ink. Gold #e8cf8b is the site's foil peak. Oxblood #9a2626 is the only new colour, and it is the colour of Uppland runestone paint.

    Sizing. XS to 3XL on everything. Count the team, add 20 percent, order once.

    Research notes

    Szpajdel's logos pay allegiance to bilateral symmetry, monochrome and ornamental decoration, and he has said readability is essential even in the most tangled ones. Valnoir of Metastazis says a cover whose concept fits in one sentence has already won. Jannicke Wiese-Hansen drew the first Immortal logo by hand as a tattoo apprentice in Bergen. The classic formats hold across the catalogue: Darkthrone's Transilvanian Hunger with the cover front and Ferdasyn back, Emperor's Nightside Eclipse longsleeve with the title alone on the back, Bathory's longsleeve with the cover front, goat back and both sleeves printed, Mayhem's longsleeve in white and purple. Necrolord painted both Emperor's and Dissection's defining covers, and Dissection's survives as a back patch. Deafheaven sold the Sunbather typeface as a font. "Sólhvarf" is attested in Cleasby-Vigfusson, cited from Grettis saga. The sunwheel caution comes from the 2025 Danzig shirt. Grok guidance comes from the 2026 Imagine guides: quote exact words, keep them short, use Quality mode for text, and tell it to add no other text.
