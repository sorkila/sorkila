# Grok Imagine prompts

Settings: Quality mode. Art-only 3:4, flat lay 16:9, on-body 4:5, macro 1:1. Four generations per prompt, keep one. Inspect every character. Tracklist, token legend and sleeve text never render: generate art without them and typeset in Figma.

Fix a render (upload and edit): `Change the text to read exactly "SÓLHVARF". Keep the lettering style, placement, colours and everything else identical.`

Reject any render with a skull, pentagram, inverted cross, sun wheel, horned helmet or recognisable band logo. Prompt again, do not retouch.
