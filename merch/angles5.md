# Angles 5: further out (2026-10-27, "push a bit more on cool concepts")

Gjenfødt is the band from here on (Erik, 2026-10-27), Sólhvarf the record, Gjenreist the one alternative name kept. Eleven concepts that leave the metal shirt behind for a moment.

## tarot · F1 · The Sun card

```
Isolated t-shirt print artwork on a flat pure black background, no garment, no mockup, centred, 3:4 portrait. Subject: a tarot card drawn like a 1909 Rider-Waite plate, thick black outlines and flat colour in bone, oxblood red and gold: a large flat gold sun disc with a calm face and thin straight rays upward only, below it a child riding a white horse past a low stone wall, tall birch trees instead of sunflowers behind the wall, a red banner flying. At the top the roman numeral "XIX", at the bottom in a simple serif the word "GJENFØDT" in a white panel. Printed slightly worn. No other text.
```

## urnes · F2 · Stave church carving

```
Isolated t-shirt print artwork on a flat pure black background, no garment, no mockup, centred, 3:4 portrait, one bone-white ink. Subject: an Urnes-style Viking wood carving, a tall portal of interlaced slender beasts and vines biting each other in long elegant loops, carved relief drawn as fine line art, and in the centre of the portal a flat gold sun disc half risen over a carved ridge. Across the top of the portal the word "GJENFØDT" in capitals shaped like carved runic-era Latin letters. Eleventh-century Norwegian, precise, no skulls. No other text.
```

## topo · F5 · Contour lines

```
Isolated t-shirt print artwork on a flat pure black background, no garment, no mockup, centred, 3:4 portrait, bone-white ink with one gold. Subject: a topographic map of a Norwegian fjord drawn only in thin contour lines, hundreds of them, the lines bunching tight on the steep walls and spreading wide in the valley, the fjord water left as pure black, and a flat gold sun disc placed on the water where the fjord opens, with thin rays upward only. Small map text "GJENFØDT" in a thin spaced sans-serif at the bottom left with a tiny scale bar. Precise, cartographic. No other text.
```

## futhark · F6 · Younger Futhark

## gitlog · F8 · The commit log

```
Isolated t-shirt print artwork on a flat pure black background, no garment, no mockup, centred, 3:4 portrait. Subject: a tall column of about twenty lines of monospace terminal text in bone white, each line a short seven-character hash in gold followed by a short commit message, the lines near the bottom reading like "burn the old world", "everything gets rewritten", "tokens in the dark", "components of bone", "the migration", "pixel perfect or death", "the night before launch", and the topmost line "the sun turns", and above the column a flat gold sun disc rising out of the text with thin rays upward only.
```
