# Angles 6: the deep round (night of 2026-10-27)

Research first, then concepts. The lore that fits a rebirth record is already in the Eddas: Vafþrúðnismál 47, the sun bears a daughter before the wolf takes her, and the daughter rides her mother's course after the gods fall. Völuspá 59, the earth rises green from the sea a second time, an eagle over the waterfall. Vafþrúðnismál 45, Líf and Lífþrasir, the two who hid in the wood and come out into the first dawn. Then the Norwegian canon: Kittelsen's Soria Moria (1900), the golden castle far away; Munch's The Sun (1911) in the Oslo aula; Darkthrone's photocopied cover; the mørketid words (solverv, solsnu, blåtimen, soldagen). And print craft: 100% cotton for discharge, one to four spot colours, the garment is a free colour, halftones at about 55 lpi. Band is Gjenfødt on all. The sun is a disc. Two renders each, keep the better.

## soldotter · D1 · Sól's daughter

```

Subject, drawn as a dense 19th-century wood engraving: at the bottom a great wolf with its jaws closed around a dark sun disc, only a thin crescent of the old disc showing, snow and dead birches around it; above, rising out of the wolf's back like dawn, a second smaller flat gold sun disc with thin straight rays upward only, clean and untouched. Between them a thin band of runic-era Latin capitals reading "GJENFØDT". Grim at the bottom, calm at the top. No skulls, pentagrams, inverted crosses, sun wheels, horned helmets or existing band logos.
```
