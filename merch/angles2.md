# Angles 2: names and the extreme end (2026-10-27, "more extreme as well")

Erik: "more on other names as well, wider, more for me to choose from, more extreme." Two passes. NAMES: ten alternatives to Sólhvarf, each drawn as a logo in a different school so the name and the lettering get judged together. EXTREME: all-over prints, war-metal thickets, doom scale, glitch, and the far minimal end. Same rules: the sun is a disc, no banned symbols, no company logo on the front.

## nedbrent · N1 · Name · Norwegian "burned down" · War metal thicket

```
Isolated t-shirt print artwork on a flat pure black background, no garment, no mockup, centred, 3:4 portrait. Subject: a chaotic, bilaterally symmetric war metal band logo reading "NEDBRENT", the letters almost lost in a thicket of charred branches, barbed thorns and dripping ink, a small flat gold sun disc at the very centre half buried in the thicket. Ink: bone white with one gold disc. Style: hand-inked, extremely dense, 1990s Canadian war metal flyer, unreadable at first glance. No additional text. No skulls, pentagrams, inverted crosses, sun wheels, horned helmets or existing band logos.
```
